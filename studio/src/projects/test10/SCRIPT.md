## Facts + source
In 1925, Victor Lustig posed as a French official and convinced scrap-metal dealers the Eiffel Tower was to be dismantled and sold. André Poisson paid; embarrassed, he did not report the fraud. Lustig returned and tried again; the second mark alerted police. Sources: Encyclopaedia Britannica, “Victor Lustig” (https://www.britannica.com/biography/Victor-Lustig); Smithsonian Magazine, “The Man Who Sold the Eiffel Tower” (https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/man-who-sold-eiffel-tower-180956416/).
